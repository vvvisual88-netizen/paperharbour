Meet our first titles

A few useful answers

Before you turn the page.

Are these physical books?

No. These are English-language PDF downloads. The covers illustrate the digital titles; no printed book is shipped.

How will I receive my purchase?

Purchase through the title’s linked checkout provider and access your PDF through that provider. All three titles use Gumroad.

What do I need to read or print a PDF?

A device with a PDF reader. You can read on screen, or use a printer for worksheets, roles and voting aids. No dedicated game app is needed. Check the sample before deciding whether the format suits you.

Which title can I enjoy on my own?

The Case Library is for solo solving or friends working together. The Missing Hour is a solo or cooperative mystery gamebook with no host required. Someone Here Is an Impostor needs 4–8 active players and one additional non-playing Keeper: 5–9 people in total.

Which titles can I buy now?

All three titles are available through their Gumroad purchase buttons. Prices are displayed on each title.

Can I try a sample?

The Case Library and Someone Here Is an Impostor have free PDFs in their detail views. The Missing Hour has no free sample available yet. The Impostor starter contains secret Keeper pages; the Keeper should open it first.
